# PISA 2022 real-data execution blocker

At 2026-10-20, OECD's 2022 student SPSS archive is 682,364,259 bytes. The remote execution environment timed out during data acquisition. No substitute or manufactured dataset was used. Official data: https://webfs.oecd.org/pisa2022/index.html . Full design-based student-level analysis, valid Fay-BRR standard errors, country coverage and measurement comparability remain pending. Do not claim this project is complete.
