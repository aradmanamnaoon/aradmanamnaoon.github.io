01 / EXPLORATORY

The file format matters

What we found → 722 records and 36 variables parse correctly only with semicolon delimiters; comma decimals require a separate option.

What to do → Import using the source-specific delimiter and decimal configuration.

02 / EXPLORATORY

Encoded missingness changes the audit

What we found → The file contains zero literal nulls but 50 -9 cells spread across 20 records.

What to do → Preserve -9 and verify its meaning against the published codebook before recoding.

03 / EXPLORATORY

Internal consistency is not validation

What we found → The raw-direction 12-item HA03 coefficient alpha is 0.248 for 711 complete cases under a -9-unavailable sensitivity assumption.

What to do → Obtain scale keys and investigate multidimensionality before reporting a wellbeing score.

04 / EXPLORATORY

Age-related patterns are exploratory

What we found → Age spans 12 to 85 and a raw HA03 item mean differs modestly across an explicitly chosen age-40 split.

What to do → Avoid clinical or causal conclusions until variable definitions and scoring are verified.

DATA LIMITATIONS · UNRESOLVED
  • The study dataset is published with a separate codebook, but the codebook was not part of the uploaded files and the original variable labels are not embedded in CSV.
  • The -9 code appears in multiple questions; the sensitivity calculation assumes it denotes an unavailable response, but the original CSV remains unchanged.
  • HA03 reliability is computed on raw directions and complete cases; its score semantics and reverse coding are not established. No clinical diagnosis or outcome score is inferred.
  • The survey has no participant identifier or repeated-measures index; one row per respondent is assumed, not externally verified.
  • Observational cross-sectional results cannot support causal claims.

Published as Schuster et al. (2021), PsychArchives CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.23668/psycharchives.4451. Only aggregate derivatives are included in this local review ZIP.
