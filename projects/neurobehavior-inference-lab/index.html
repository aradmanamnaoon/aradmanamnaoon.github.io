Neurobehavior Inference Lab: Working-memory load, reaction time and trial-level behavioral statistics

An open, statistics-first investigation of OpenNeuro ds000030 (UCLA CNP). Explore the question, planned analysis, sample-design requirements and limitations, while numerical results remain pending.

01 · Context

Research question

How do working-memory load and delay relate to speed and accuracy within repeated behavioral task trials?

02 · Methods

Approach

  • Read BIDS behavioral events and phenotype metadata, not MRI volumes.
  • Validate response correctness and prespecified reaction-time windows.
  • Fit participant-intercept Gaussian/log-RT and binomial mixed-effects models.
  • Quantify participant heterogeneity and rerun with alternative outlier cutoffs.

03 · Evaluation

Reported results

  • Current release is a research implementation draft, not a completed empirical study.
  • No verified sample sizes, model coefficients, effect sizes, statistical figures or p-values are available.
  • R execution, data licensing/codebook validation, diagnostics and independent numerical checks must pass before substantive findings are added.

04 · Scope

Limitations

No fMRI analysis, psychiatric diagnosis, reliability validation or causal claim is established. The trial exclusion window and task timing definitions need independent validation. Models and R tests have not been run. All current figures and estimates are withheld pending real-data execution and validation.
