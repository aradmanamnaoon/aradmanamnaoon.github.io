Psychemetrics Atlas: Latent dimensions, item coverage and the limits of personality measurement

An open, statistics-first investigation of SAPA public personality questionnaires. Explore the question, planned analysis, sample-design requirements and limitations, while numerical results remain pending.

01 · Context

Research question

How stable are exploratory personality item dimensions when respondents encounter only subsets of a large questionnaire?

02 · Methods

Approach

  • Audit item codebooks, scales, reverse-coding and response coverage.
  • Assess sparse pairwise overlap before estimating polychoric correlations.
  • Explore oblique factor solutions, eigenstructure and item-level sensitivity.
  • Separate unkeyed descriptive scale consistency from validated trait reliability.

03 · Evaluation

Reported results

  • Current release is a research implementation draft, not a completed empirical study.
  • No verified sample sizes, model coefficients, effect sizes, statistical figures or p-values are available.
  • R execution, data licensing/codebook validation, diagnostics and independent numerical checks must pass before substantive findings are added.

04 · Scope

Limitations

SAPA is a volunteer, nonprobability sample with matrix-sampled items. The scoring key and cross-group invariance are not yet verified; no validated trait scale, confirmatory factor analysis or item-response-theory result is claimed. All current figures and estimates are withheld pending real-data execution and validation.
