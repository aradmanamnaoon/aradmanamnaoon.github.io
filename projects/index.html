Flagship model · Persian NLP · From-scratch training

Persian GPT — 110M Transformer From Scratch

This project demonstrates an end-to-end low-resource language-model workflow for Persian, from corpus preparation through tokenizer design, training, and held-out evaluation.

110.4Mparameters
244Mtraining tokens
14.52held-out perplexity
  • 855,724 cleaned Persian Wikipedia documents
  • Custom 32K byte-pair encoding tokenizer
  • PyTorch decoder-only Transformer
  • N-gram baselines and held-out perplexity

The reported perplexity is specific to the held-out corpus and tokenizer. It is not a measure of factuality, instruction following, or downstream task performance.

Medical AI · 3D MRI · Deep learning

3D Cardiac MRI Segmentation

The project investigates voxel-level cardiac structure segmentation from volumetric MRI, a setting where models must use three-dimensional spatial context.

0.8821best validation Dice
100training epochs
3D U-Netarchitecture
  • Medical Segmentation Decathlon Task02 Heart
  • PyTorch and MONAI preprocessing
  • 3D U-Net with DiceCELoss and AdamW
  • Mixed precision and sliding-window inference

The reported validation Dice is from this project’s benchmark split. The system has not been clinically validated and is not a clinical decision-support tool.
