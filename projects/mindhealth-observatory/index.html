MindHealth Observatory: Depression screening, population inequality and survey-weighted inference

An open, statistics-first investigation of CDC NHANES 2017–2018. Explore the question, planned analysis, sample-design requirements and limitations, while numerical results remain pending.

01 · Context

Research question

How does the prevalence of elevated depressive symptoms differ across socioeconomic and demographic strata once the NHANES sample design is respected?

02 · Methods

Approach

  • Validate all nine PHQ-9 symptoms and survey eligibility before scoring.
  • Use 2-year MEC weights, masked strata and PSU identifiers for design-based estimates.
  • Estimate weighted prevalence, survey-weighted GLMs, design-aware confidence intervals and nonlinear income sensitivity.
  • Audit complete-case selection and report all weights, denominators and exclusion rules.

03 · Evaluation

Reported results

  • Current release is a research implementation draft, not a completed empirical study.
  • No verified sample sizes, model coefficients, effect sizes, statistical figures or p-values are available.
  • R execution, data licensing/codebook validation, diagnostics and independent numerical checks must pass before substantive findings are added.

04 · Scope

Limitations

Cross-sectional survey estimates are associations, not evidence of causality. PHQ-9 screening is not clinical diagnosis. Multiple imputation and cross-cycle comparisons are not implemented in the staged source. All current figures and estimates are withheld pending real-data execution and validation.
