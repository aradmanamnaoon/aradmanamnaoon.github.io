04

Agentic AI · Multi-agent systems · RAG

AI Research Assistant Platform

A production-style research assistant built around a LangGraph multi-agent workflow. A planner routes work across retrieval, research, and tool execution; compatible branches run in parallel, a synthesizer produces citation-grounded answers, and a reflector can send weak drafts through a bounded refinement loop. The platform adds ChromaDB RAG, persistent SQLite state, LangSmith tracing and evaluation, REST and SSE APIs with FastAPI, and Docker-ready deployment.

A little more about what I do.

A quick overview of my work across data science, deep learning, computer vision and medical imaging, NLP and language models, LLM fine-tuning, RAG, agentic AI, and software and mobile development.

What kind of AI systems have you built?
I've built multiple agentic AI and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, including tool-using workflows, retrieval pipelines, vector search, stateful interactions, and LLM orchestration. I've also built deep learning and traditional machine-learning systems.
Do you train deep learning models from scratch?
Yes. I've built and trained many deep learning models from scratch across computer vision, NLP, medical imaging, and other applied domains. Persian GPT is one public example: a 110.4M-parameter decoder-only transformer trained from scratch on Persian text.
What is your strongest area in computer vision?
Computer vision is one of my strongest areas, with a particular specialization in medical imaging. My work includes image segmentation, 3D medical-image pipelines, CNN-based modeling, preprocessing, inference, visualization, and task-specific evaluation.
Do you fine-tune LLMs and other pretrained models?
Yes. I've fine-tuned many models for specialized tasks using full fine-tuning and parameter-efficient methods such as LoRA, QLoRA, and PEFT. My public fine-tuned models and adapters are available on Hugging Face.
What experience do you have with NLP and language models?
My NLP work includes language-model training from scratch, tokenizer and dataset preparation, transformer fine-tuning, structured information extraction, embeddings, semantic search, RAG, and LLM-based agent systems.
Do you develop mobile applications with Flutter?
Yes. I develop cross-platform mobile applications with Flutter for Android and iOS.
Where can I see your source code?
My public code, experiments, and engineering projects are available on GitHub.
Where can I find your models, fine-tuned checkpoints, and datasets?
I publish models, fine-tuned checkpoints, adapters, and datasets on Hugging Face.
Which projects best represent your current skill set?
AI Research Assistant Platform demonstrates multi-agent orchestration, RAG, LangGraph, LangSmith observability, FastAPI, testing, persistence, and Docker-ready engineering. Persian GPT demonstrates transformer training and NLP from scratch. 3D Cardiac MRI Segmentation demonstrates deep learning and medical computer vision.
