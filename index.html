I'm a data scientist and machine learning engineer who turns messy, real-world data into products people rely on. My work spans the full ML lifecycle — from exploratory analysis and prototyping to training, evaluating, and shipping models to production.

More recently, I've focused on agentic AI engineering: building stateful, tool-using LLM systems with LangGraph, instrumenting them with LangSmith, and taking them from notebook to deployed, observable product. I care most about the intersection of solid engineering, thoughtful statistics, and clear communication.
